## What is Datapit?

**Datapit** is a free website for players of **GPRO (Grand Prix Racing Online)**. You sign in with your GPRO API token, it reads your game data, and it tells you what to do before the next race: which setup to use, which tyres and how many stops, which parts to replace, and which training to pick.

It is not a simulator. Every rule is checked against real race data from many managers, and when a number is only an estimate, Datapit says so.

## What it does

| | |
|---|---|
| **Car setup** | Uses your practice laps to find the right setup for the track, the weather, your driver and your car, and shows how far you can be from the ideal before it costs time. [Read more](https://datapit.eu/gpro-setup) |
| **Tyre strategy and pit stops** | Compares the compounds for the next race. Change the stops and the litres and see at once what it does to your total time. Includes a rain planner and a choice of fuel safety margin. [Read more](https://datapit.eu/gpro-tyre-strategy) |
| **Car wear and parts plan** | Projects the wear of every part race by race to the end of the season, so you can plan replacements and upgrades before a part reaches the failure limit. |
| **Driver training plan** | Plan training, test laps and expected finishing positions over up to five seasons, and see how overall and energy develop. [Read more](https://datapit.eu/gpro-driver-training) |
| **Race report** | After each race: what Datapit had forecast, what happened, where the seconds went, and how your pit stops compare with your group. [Read more](https://datapit.eu/gpro-race-report) |
| **Season economy** | A forecast of cash race by race, with sponsors, salaries, prizes and the cost of your plan. |

## Why use it

- **Free, no email, no password.** You only need your GPRO API token.
- **Read-only.** The token lets Datapit read your game data; it cannot change anything on your account.
- **Built on real races.** The models are calibrated on thousands of stints from real races of many managers, not only yours, and they are re-measured as new races arrive.
- **Honest about uncertainty.** Estimates are labelled as estimates.
- **Seven languages:** English, Italiano, Português (Brasil), Español, Français, Deutsch, Русский.

## Getting started

1. Open **[datapit.eu](https://datapit.eu/)**.
2. Copy your API token from the GPRO app (the API access page of the menu) and paste it in Datapit.
3. Follow the path of the week: update your data, then training, tests, practice, qualifying and race strategy.
